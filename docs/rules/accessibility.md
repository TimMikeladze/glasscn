# Accessibility rules

1. **Contrast is checked, not assumed.** For every shipped palette × material in both schemes, `legibilityReport()` must show text and secondary text ≥ 4.5:1 on subtle, default and strong panes, the accent's label ≥ 4.5:1, the accent against the ground ≥ 3:1, and the labels of prominent glass and of tinted / destructive buttons ≥ 4.5:1 (`prominentLabel`, `tintedLabel`, `destructiveLabel`; the `*-ink` text colours carry `INK_ACCENT` = 30% accent for this). `glass-theme.test.ts` tests this. Inside panes, text uses `--glass-label` / `--glass-label-secondary` (the panes remap shadcn's foreground tokens). Lower a frost opacity only with that test green.
2. **Focus is always visible, including in forced colours.** Every interactive part uses `glass-focus` (or `glass-focus-inset` for flush rows): a two-tone ring: a `--ring` outline of `--glass-ring-width` with a 2px halo in the pane's colour between it and the control, so it shows on any glass and follows the radius. Outlines survive forced colours. Never `outline-none`.
3. **Radix does the semantics.** Use Radix primitives for anything with roles, keyboard handling or focus management (switch, tabs, dialog, menu, slider, toggle group). Don't rebuild them from divs.
4. **Every control has a name.** Icon-only buttons take `aria-label`; sliders and segmented controls take `aria-label` (or a `Label`).
5. **Data has text.** `ActivityRings` and charts carry `role="img"` and an `aria-label` describing the values; heatmap cells have titles/labels.
6. **Motion respects the user.** `prefers-reduced-motion` stills the aurora and every `data-glass-motion` element, zeroes press stretch / grow and the knob lens, and turns materialise and morph-from-trigger into a short cross-fade; the `still` motion preset does it for everyone. Reduce transparency and increase contrast also zero the specular rim and the press glow.
7. **No information by colour alone.** Kept/missed, trends and states pair colour with text or an icon.
8. **Fallbacks stay readable.** Without `backdrop-filter`, panes become nearly opaque. Test with the `matte` material.
